What Is Migraine?

Migraine is a neurological disorder that can cause recurring moderate to severe headaches.

Common Symptoms

  • Throbbing headache
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Sensitivity to light
  • Sensitivity to sound

Visual Aura

Some people experience temporary visual symptoms, such as flashing lights, zigzag lines, or blind spots.
